# Friend Notifications

The World of Tanks mod shows alerts when friends come online or go offline.

## Language

**Friend**:
A player on the local player's World of Tanks friends list. Every friend is eligible for presence notifications.

**Presence notification**:
A brief popup on the left side of the local player's screen when a friend's status changes between online and offline. Popups appear while the hangar or battle interface is visible. The friends list's initial status snapshot is silent.

**Battle notification**:
A presence notification shown below the left team panel during battle. When that panel is hidden, the notification appears beside battle chat if chat is available.

**Notification stack**:
A group of up to three presence notifications visible at the same time.

**Pending notification**:
A presence notification waiting for a slot in the notification stack. Only the latest pending notification for each friend is kept. The remaining notifications appear in the order their status changes were received.
